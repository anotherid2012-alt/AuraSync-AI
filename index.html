AuraSync AI · Privacy

Privacy Policy

How the AuraSync AI school attendance and volunteer-check project may handle information.

Effective date: 28 October 2026
Important before use: AuraSync AI is a school-project system that may process information about students, including facial images. It should only be used after the responsible school has reviewed the system, approved its data practices, informed families, and obtained any consent or authorization required by applicable rules. Do not use it to make consequential decisions about a student without meaningful human review.
At a glance
  • Information may include student details, attendance records, camera images or face encodings, volunteer-check results, and WhatsApp messages sent to the service.
  • The system is intended for school attendance and school-rule volunteer checks, not advertising or selling personal information.
  • Access should be limited to authorized school/project administrators.
  • Retention and deletion must be managed by the responsible administrator; this policy does not promise automatic deletion.

1. Who is responsible?

AuraSync AI is a student-built project associated with the Aura Boys project team. For a school deployment, the school or organization operating the system should identify the responsible adult or administrator and confirm who controls the information. The team should not independently decide how student information is used.

2. Information that may be processed

  • Student records: name, roll number, class, section, and parent/guardian contact details if entered by an authorized administrator.
  • Attendance records: attendance status, date, time, and associated student identifier.
  • Face and camera data: camera frames or photographs used for recognition, and face encodings or model files used to identify registered students.
  • Volunteer checks: images or observations and results relating to checks such as uniform, ID card, shoes, socks, hair, or nails, if those features are enabled.
  • WhatsApp: the sender's phone number or WhatsApp identifier, message text, message identifiers, timestamps, and delivery/processing information needed to receive and reply to messages.
  • Technical information: error logs and basic system/network information used to diagnose and secure the service.

Only information necessary for an approved school purpose should be collected. Do not enter medical details, passwords, or other unnecessary sensitive information into WhatsApp or the system.

3. Why information is used

Information may be used to register authorized students, recognize registered faces for attendance, maintain attendance history, support approved volunteer checks, respond to incoming WhatsApp messages, and troubleshoot or secure the system. It must not be repurposed for advertising, public posting, profiling unrelated to the school purpose, or unrelated monitoring.

4. Where information is processed and shared

The project is designed to run on a Raspberry Pi and may use a separate computer on the same local network for AI image analysis. Depending on the actual deployment configuration, images or prompts may be sent to that local AI computer for processing. Administrators should verify the network configuration and restrict access to trusted devices.

If WhatsApp integration is enabled, messages are handled through Meta's WhatsApp services. Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policies. The project may share the minimum information needed with the school staff who administer the system and with service providers technically necessary to operate an enabled feature.

AuraSync AI should not sell personal information or share it for third-party advertising. Do not assume that every component is local-only: check the deployed settings and network traffic before making that claim.

5. Facial images and students under 18

Facial images and face encodings can be sensitive personal information. Access should be restricted, camera use should be clearly communicated, and non-biometric alternatives should be considered where practical. The responsible school must assess applicable requirements for children, privacy, biometric processing, and consent before enabling face recognition. Students should not be penalized solely because recognition fails or produces an uncertain result; an authorized adult should verify attendance.

6. WhatsApp messages

The WhatsApp feature is intended to receive messages and send relevant replies. The current project intention is not to send automated attendance notifications to parents. If that behavior changes, this policy and the school notice should be updated first. Avoid sending student records or other sensitive information in a reply unless the school has explicitly approved the purpose, recipient verification, and safeguards.

7. Retention and deletion

Attendance records, registered student photos, face encodings, and logs may remain stored until an authorized administrator deletes them or applies the school's retention schedule. WhatsApp message information may also be retained in system logs or by WhatsApp/Meta according to their own policies. The deployment administrator should document retention periods, periodically review stored data, securely delete information that is no longer needed, and keep backups under the same access restrictions.

8. Security

Reasonable safeguards should include strong device and account passwords, limited administrator access, protected environment files and API tokens, updated software, network access restrictions, secure backups, and avoiding public exposure of the Raspberry Pi or its database. Never publish access tokens, App Secrets, student photographs, face models, attendance databases, or private logs in a public repository. No system can guarantee perfect security.

9. Choices and privacy requests

A student or parent/guardian should contact the responsible school administrator to ask what information is held, request correction, ask about retention, or request deletion where applicable. The school should verify the requester's authority and respond under applicable rules. If the system is not approved for a student, the school should provide an appropriate alternative attendance process.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy should be updated when data collected, AI processing, WhatsApp behavior, service providers, or retention practices change. The responsible administrator should review significant changes before deploying them and communicate the updated policy to affected people.

11. Contact

For questions or privacy requests, contact the adult school administrator responsible for the AuraSync AI deployment, or the Aura Boys project team through the school. Before using this page as the official policy, the responsible school should add a monitored contact email or other reliable contact method and confirm the information above matches the actual deployment.

Transparency note: This is a project privacy-policy draft, not legal advice or a certification of compliance. The system operator must confirm that it matches the software, actual data flows, retention settings, and school approvals before using it as the official policy.
